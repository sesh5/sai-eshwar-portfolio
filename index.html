Project 1 of 11 NourishAI · 2026 · AI product

Try asking a food app for “high-protein, under 500 calories, not fried.”

Most apps just hear “food.” And even a clever one would fail, because restaurant menus don't list calories or protein in the first place.

So I made the data exist. An LLM pipeline generates and enriches every dish with calories, macros and tags and loads it into Supabase. Then Claude Haiku turns your sentence into rules and Postgres ranks the dishes. The API key never touches the browser.

Result: type a craving in plain English, get ranked dishes with the numbers attached.

Project 2 of 11 Content Arena (formerly RivalRadar) · 2025 · AI product

“Who is my competition?” Every YouTuber asks. Subscriber counts do not answer.

The giant channel might be coasting. The one quietly winning your audience might be a tenth its size.

Content Arena finds the channels that are actually comparable and scores them on engagement normalised for size, so a big channel doesn't automatically look like a strong one.

Result: a defensible answer to who your real rivals are and which levers separate you, instead of gut feel.

Project 3 of 11 Decoding the 2026 Tamil Nadu Election · Data analysis

8,000+ rows of election data, two cycles, and names that refused to match.

All 234 constituencies, for a newsroom that wanted one clear story. The newsroom was made up for a Codebasics challenge. The data came straight from the Election Commission of India.

I cleaned and reconciled both cycles in Python, computed seat flips, vote-share swings and margins, and shipped seven charts and an eleven-slide deck.

Result: 163 of 234 seats changed party (69.7%), and a new entrant took 35.1% of the statewide vote.

Project 4 of 11 Forage PM Job Simulation · 2026 · Product management

Downloads up. Players leaving. Three teams, three opinions, zero agreement.

Legends of Aether, a strategy RPG, was a textbook case of one dashboard that everyone reads differently.

I picked one KPI per question and argued why the near miss was wrong: 7-day retention over DAU, because DAU mixes veterans in and hides new players quietly leaving. Then a 7-step plan that split one deck into three: development, marketing and leadership.

Worked example from my brief: 10,000 installs, 3,000 back on day 7, so 30% retention.

Project 5 of 11 AI Voice Agent for L1 Support · 2025

Support agents answering the same question for the hundredth time today.

Tier 1 questions have known answers, and a person still picks up every one of them.

I built a voice agent with ElevenLabs and N8N, curated its knowledge base from scratch, and grounded every answer with RAG, so it answers from the knowledge base instead of improvising.

Result: Tier 1 answered autonomously. Humans keep the calls that need judgment.

Project 6 of 11 Fathom × Freshdesk Automation · 2025

After every call: copy the summary, paste it into the ticket, repeat forever.

Slow, easy to get wrong, and the first thing skipped on a busy day.

An N8N pipeline now does it alone. The call ends, a webhook fires, and the AI summary and recording land in the right Freshdesk ticket, with routing logic for the edge cases.

Result: call notes reach the right ticket without anyone touching them.

Project 7 of 11 IoV Security with ASCON · 2023 to 2024 · Research

Cars on 5G share safety-critical data on hardware too small for heavy encryption.

Strong encryption is too slow for that hardware, and fast encryption is too weak to trust with a moving car.

I ran the ASCON lightweight cipher on Raspberry Pi boards over MQTT, attacked the setup with DoS and man-in-the-middle simulations, and trained an ensemble ML classifier to catch the intrusions.

Result: 99.95% DoS detection accuracy, NPCR 99.42%, and a Springer Nature paper, which comes up again in a moment.

Project 8 of 11 Crop Yield Predictor · 2023 · Machine learning

Crops chosen by tradition, while the soil holds the actual answer.

Soil type, moisture, pH and crop history say a lot about what will grow, if anyone actually reads them.

I engineered features from Kaggle agricultural datasets and trained a Random Forest, validated on held-out data, that turns soil readings into a crop recommendation.

Result: 98% validation accuracy.

Project 9 of 11 Smart Granary System · 2024 · IoT

Stored grain spoils quietly, and nobody can watch a granary all night.

Temperature and CO2 drift, and the first alert is usually the smell.

I built a system that tracks temperature and CO2 against set thresholds around the clock and flags action the moment conditions drift.

Result: storage kept in range with far less manual monitoring, and fewer losses.

Project 10 of 11 Posture Correcting Device · 2024 · Hardware

You are probably slouching right now.

It builds up slowly over long desk sessions, and you only notice once your back starts hurting.

I built a wearable around an Arduino Nano with an accelerometer and gyroscope, plus Embedded C that spots misalignment in real time and fires a vibration motor.

Result: a short buzz the moment you slump, so you fix it before it hurts.

Project 11 of 11 Federated Learning for D2D Networks · 2023 · Research internship

Devices that must decide how to talk to each other, with nobody in charge.

In a device-to-device network, each phone has to trade off energy, distance and signal quality on its own, without a central coordinator.

Onsite at the University of Cyprus, I simulated a decentralised network in NS3, clustered its nodes four different ways, and trained a neural network to pick transmission modes.

Result: a working mode-selection model for the group's federated learning research, and a crash course in working inside an international research team.
