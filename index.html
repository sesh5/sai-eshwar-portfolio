About

I am the kind of person who cannot leave a problem alone. When I see someone fighting a clunky process or quietly working around something broken, I want to understand exactly why it hurts, and then build something that makes that pain disappear.

That curiosity is what pulls me toward building products. I love sitting with people, asking the slightly annoying follow up questions, and digging until the real problem surfaces underneath the one they first described. Then comes the part I enjoy most: turning that insight into something real and useful, whether it is an automation, a dashboard, or a working prototype I can put in front of someone the same week.

Tenacity is the other half of it. I would rather ship a rough first version, watch how people actually use it, and improve from there than wait around for a perfect plan. Across research, AI tooling, and enterprise work, the thread is always the same: find a problem worth solving and build my way to the answer.
